## Resources listed in the proposal but not used

- **Indus Script IM77 platform (RMRL, indusscript.in):** no downloadable export of its texts could be found (checked during M2). Mahadevan's numbering enters the project through the IM-417-150 images and the CISI crosswalk instead.
- **AI-EPIGRAPHY toolkit:** not used. Frequency, collocation and transition statistics were implemented directly in M2, so that every definition is visible and testable.

## Derived data

- `data/processed/crosswalk.csv`: built by `python src/common/signs.py`.
- `src/m3_masking/artifacts/splits.json`: the M3 train/validation/test split. It is not committed because it contains corpus sequences. `run_all.py` rebuilds it identically (seed 0, verified).
- Synthetic training data for M1 and the engine's synthetic strips are generated by the scripts in `src/m1_vision/` and `src/engine/`.

## Third-party images inside this repository

- `reports/figures/*Steatite_stamp_seal*`: derived from "Steatite stamp seal Indus script - BM 1912,0629.1" by Zunkir, Wikimedia Commons, **CC BY-SA 4.0**. Derived figures are shared under the same licence.
- `reports/presentations/Indus_Epigraphy_Milestone_1_Presentation.pptx`: contains figures derived from the Wikimedia Commons photographs and Indus-Seal-Dataset images listed above. Licences are per image (see `data/raw/real_seals/attribution.csv`). The Indus-Seal-Dataset images carry no stated licence.
- `reports/deck_figures/` is **excluded** by `.gitignore` for the same reason.
